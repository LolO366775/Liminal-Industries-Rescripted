Here be the instructions for how to use this effectively:

To get setup, make the base of your local repository the modpack folder of a build of Liminal Industries Rewritten from curseforge. 
I would recommend having 2 in curseforge, 1 normal modpack and 1 duplicate that is used for development.

If you are confused about how to do this you can ask me in the Liminal industries Rewritten discord, My username in discord is LolO

To start making changes, make a branch on the public github and on your private github.
Make your edits in that branch on your private github, then whenever you finish something, commit your changes and then push them to your branch on the public github.
